Joining terms

Straightforward, on purpose.

No joining fee, on any plan. Membership runs monthly and rolls over automatically. You're not locked into a year you didn't ask for.

If you're heading out of Bengaluru for a stretch, or carrying an injury, freeze your membership for up to two months a year at no charge. Tell the front desk before your next billing date.

Students and corporate teams can ask at the front desk about reduced rates. These are assessed case by case, so bring your student ID or company details when you ask.

FAQ

Common questions.

How do I cancel my membership?

Give us 30 days' notice, by email or a note at the front desk. There's no cancellation fee.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Every member can freeze for up to two months a year, for travel, injury, or anything else. Let the front desk know before your next billing date.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit or credit card, and cash at the front desk. Monthly billing runs automatically once you're set up on UPI or card.

Can I try before I join?

Yes. Ask about a trial session at the front desk or through the contact page. We'll walk you through the floor and a class before you commit to anything.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes, single-visit day passes are available at the front desk for anyone not ready to commit to a monthly plan.

Is there a couple or family rate?

Ask at the front desk. We offer a reduced per-person rate when two or more from the same household join together.
