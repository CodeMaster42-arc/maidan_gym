Getting here

Finding the floor.

01

By car

Street parking is available along Dodda Banaswadi Main Road and the side lanes near Shamanna Layout. Two-wheelers can pull up directly outside the building.

02

By bus or metro

Regular bus services run along the main road with stops within walking distance. Coming by metro, plan for a short auto or cab ride to finish the last stretch.

03

Landmark

We're on the 3rd floor, set back from the road near the Shamanna Layout and Subaiya Reddy Layout junction. Look for the Maidan signage above the entrance.

FAQ

Before you visit.

Do I need to book a trial?

It isn't strictly required, but it helps. Send us a message or call ahead so a coach knows to expect you and can walk you through the floor properly.

What should I bring for a first visit?

Comfortable training clothes, indoor shoes, a water bottle, and a small towel cover most first sessions. We'll handle the rest.

Is there parking?

Yes, street parking is available along the main road, with space for two-wheelers directly outside the building.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes, single-visit day passes are available for anyone not ready to commit to a monthly plan. Ask through the form above or call the front desk.
