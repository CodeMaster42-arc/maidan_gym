Who we are

Not a wellness brand.
A gym.

We are a neighbourhood gym on Dodda Banaswadi Main Road, and we have been here since 2019. No franchise playbook, no contract you need a lawyer to leave.

What we have is a floor that works: proper racks, real weight, space to move, and coaches who learn your name and your injuries. Some members come at six in the morning before a shift. Some come at nine at night after one. Both get the same room and the same attention.

Classes

Coached sessions from before dawn to late evening. Every class is capped so you get corrected, not just counted.

All classes & timetable →

Strength 60 min

Squat, press, hinge, pull. A barbell progression you actually repeat week to week, loaded to what you can hold form under. Beginners get taught the lifts from zero.

Conditioning 45 min

Short intervals on bikes, ropes, sleds and your own bodyweight. Hard for the whole forty-five minutes, scaled so a first-timer and a regular can suffer side by side.

Boxing 50 min

Stance, footwork and combinations on the bags and the pads. Technique first, sweat as a by-product. No sparring unless you ask for it and a coach agrees you are ready.

Before you come

Questions

Can I try before I join?

Yes. Your first session is free, class or gym floor, no card needed. Call ahead on 077603 44646 so a coach is free to show you around.

I have never trained before. Is this the wrong gym?

No. A good share of our members started from nothing here. Everyone gets an induction that covers the floor, the machines and how to not hurt yourself in week one.

Do I need to book classes in advance?

Classes are capped, so booking is recommended for evening slots. Mornings you can usually walk in. Call or message us to hold a spot.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Memberships run month to month and you can stop at the end of any paid month. See memberships for what each tier includes.

What should I bring on the first visit?

Indoor shoes, a towel and water. Lockers are free to use while you train, but bring your own lock if you would rather.
